## 1. Executive Summary & Governing Invariant

This specification suite defines the protocol, cryptography, economics, and `go-zenon` upgrades to store native BTC in a **BitVM2-constrained Taproot vault** on Bitcoin, represent that BTC 1:1 as **`zBTC`** (`1 base unit = 1 satoshi`, 8 decimals, `21,000,000 BTC` cap) via an atomic **Treasury Pre-Mint** `SettlementContract`, transfer `zBTC` with **0 satoshi BTC fees** via Zenon's QSR Plasma / SHA3-256 PoW capacity model, verify Zenon state via **30-day BLS12-381 `FinalityCommitteeRoot` + `StateRoot`** proofs, and offer **peg-free, zero-vault-trust PTLC adaptor swaps and Lightning submarine-swap gateways**.

### 1.1 The Governing Invariant (with Phase-2 Adjudication Addendum)

> **Consensus orders. Execution computes. Authorization admits. Settlement accounts.**
>
> **Phase-2 Adjudication Addendum:** Under `execProfile = OPTIMISTIC`, `SettlementContract` hosts deterministic, panic-free, bounded-CPU registered verifier plugins (`VerifierRegistry`, e.g., `OPTIMISTIC_BTC_SPV_V1`) **solely to adjudicate single-step fraud challenges** against `BatchCommitmentV3.traceRoot`. All plugins MUST return typed `error` values (zero `common.DealWithErr` panics) and support both 1-tx fast challenges (`<= 12 KiB` typical) and bounded Multi-Tx Witness Sessions (`UploadWitnessProof = 8,676 B` at max SMT depth 256, `T_session = 12h`, blocking `FinalizeBatch` while open). Under Phase 3 (`ZK_VALIDITY`), Settlement verifies succinct proofs only and parses zero foreign data.
>
> **Boundary Addendum:** Destination chains enforce through the adapter and hash-pinned Interop Enforcement Specification bound by `ChainBinding.enforcementSpecHash`.

## 3. End-to-End Protocol Flows

### 3.1 Peg-In: BTC → BitVM2 Taproot Vault → Atomic Treasury `zBTC`

1. **Provisional Deposit**: User derives `ProvisionalDepositScript` (`P_prov = lift_x(H_NUMS_X) + depositCommit * G`, key-path unspendable; Leaf A = `CommitteeIdentityKey`; Leaf B = `<144> OP_CSV <depositorRefundPubKey>`) committing `(chainBindingHash, assetId, zenonRecipient[0]==0x00, depositNonce, u64BE(D_bucket))` and broadcasts `DepositTx` (`<= 4,096 B`, non-coinbase, `len != 64`).
2. **Normative Two-Phase Ceremony, Transcript Attestation & Mandatory 2-Input Rollover (`R5-L8`)**:
   - **Phase 1 (Trap Commitment First)**: Each participating Operator `m` delivers verified `pk_m` signatures on BOTH `WrongDepositDisproveTx^{T1}` AND `WrongDepositDisproveTx^{T2}` across active fresh rounds of generation `g` BEFORE `Q_graph` releases any Take signature.
   - **Phase 2 (Take Release, Published Leaf DA, Transcript & Rollover)**: For operators passing Phase 1, `Q_graph` releases `Take1Tx`/`Take2Tx` signatures and signs a canonical `Q_graph` release transcript listing the exact sorted set of released Take txids (`takeTxidsSmtRoot` — `proof-format.md` V1 SMT keyed by `SHA3-256(takeTxid)` — PLUS canonical `takeTxidsBlobHash` committed alongside published leaf-set DA so any watcher can construct SMT absence proofs, `R6-L5`). The distinct Setup Committee (`CommitteeIdentityKey`) independently verifies the `Q_graph` transcript & published leaf set, signs the lock attestation `(rolloverOutpoint, eligibleOperatorsBitmask, epochId = g, takeTxidsSmtRoot, takeTxidsBlobHash)`, and broadcasts mandatory 2-input `RolloverTx_d` (`nSequence = 0xfffffffd`, Reserve `In 1`, `Out 0 = TerminalVaultUTXO_d` paying `Q_vault_d`). New deposits after gen `g+1` registration MUST carry a `g+1` graph (`R6-L4`). If unconfirmed by `h_dep + 120`, Reserve MUST double-spend `In 1` (depositor Leaf-B RBF fallback at `h_dep + 144`).
3. **SPV Proof Relay**: Relayer submits `BTC_HEADER_BATCH`, optional `BTC_TX_STREAM_CHUNK`, and `BTC_LOCK_PROOF` (`<= 7,748 B` ABI). `d_btc_asset` verifies both txs against never-pruned `ActiveChainByHeight[h]` (`K_pilot = 14`), verifies `CommitteeIdentityKey` sig, records multi-epoch `TrackedVaultRecord{ACTIVE, activeEpochs=[{g, takeTxidsSmtRoot}]}`, and emits `FOREIGN_LOCK`.
4. **Chained Optimistic Batch, Rotation & Atomic Mint**: Executor submits `BatchCommitmentV3` (monotonic `batchId`, `parentBatchId`, `PreStateRoot` chaining, contiguous `domainInputSeq`, `MaxBatchInputs = 256`). After `48h` (up to `60h` with open challenge session), `FinalizeBatch` finalizes and releases exposure. `RelayClaim` invokes `MINTED_LOCK_V1`, enforces **G-21 Latency-Aware Dynamic Reachable Fresh Capacity** (`ActiveVaultUTXOs + 1 <= Σ (ReachableRounds(now + 3.0d) - pendingReservations) - 25`, blocked if `unrotatedVaultCount > 0`; smoothly defers mints to `DeferredLockQueue` during the disclosed `~5.0d` pre-epoch window before gen `g+1` registers at day `30–32`), records per-epoch `VaultUtxoSet[outpoint_d]`, and atomically transfers `D_bucket` `zBTC` from treasury to `zenonRecipient`.

### 3.2 Feeless `zBTC` Transfers on Zenon Block-Lattice

Standard `zBTC` user transfers pay **0 satoshis BTC fee** and `21,000` base Plasma funded via refundable QSR fusion (`10 QSR` at floor price `1000`) or SHA3-256 PoW (`dev:pow/pow.go:35-47,61,118`, `31.5M` expected hashes at floor price; ~0.1–0.3s desktop native, ~2–10s mobile/WASM). Network-wide L1 capacity is `100` blocks per 10s Momentum (`~5` full send+receive user transfers/s).

### 3.3 Peg-Out: Atomic Burn → Anti-Censorship Fronting Slot → `FrontingTx` → Zenon `RELEASED` → BitVM2 Reimbursement

1. **`RequestPegOut`** (open during `ACTIVE`, `DRAINING_ROLLOVER_STOP`, AND `DRAINING_FINAL`): User sends `D_bucket` `zBTC` with `(recipientScriptPubKey, refundRecipient, timeoutHeight, pegOutFeeSats >= minOperatorFeeSats + fixedReserveFeeSats)`. `Settlement` validates `tip + 640 <= timeoutHeight <= tip + 2512 && timeoutHeight < 500_000_000`, pops an `UNASSIGNED` `outpoint_d` holding a live graph epoch `e` with `>= 1` eligible active operator having `ReachableRounds(e, now + 3.0d) - pendingReservations >= 1` (`R6-M2`), copies `rec.ChainBindingHash = outpoint_d.SetupChainBindingHash`, retains `D_bucket` `zBTC`, and appends `PEGOUT_CREATED` (`0x03`).
2. **Single-Authority, Anti-Censorship Fronting Slot (`R6-M2`, `R6-M4`, `R6-M5`) & `FrontingTx`**: An eligible operator in a live graph epoch `e` of `outpoint_d` with `ReachableRounds(e, now + 3.0d) - pendingReservations >= 1` calls `ClaimFrontingSlot(outboxId)` (requiring `VaultUtxoSet[d].status == ASSIGNED`, locking forfeitable `SlotBondZnn = 500 ZNN`, reserving 1 lane round, barring consecutive re-claim by `lastLapsedOperatorId`, and rejecting until BOTH `now >= slotClaimZenonTs + 14_400` AND `lastFinalizedTipHeight >= slotExpiryBtcHeight`!). Appends `PEGOUT_SLOT_CLAIMED` (`0x0A`); `d_btc_asset` is the single authority on slot exclusivity (`BestTipHeight + 24`, `slotClaimZenonTs + 14_400`). Operator broadcasts `FrontingTx` and submits `BTC_RELEASE_PROOF`; **`d_btc_asset` ALWAYS admits valid fronting regardless of vault trap status (`R6-M3`)**, emitting `FOREIGN_RELEASE(operatorId)`; `MINTED_RELEASE_V1` keys strictly on `FOREIGN_RELEASE.operatorId`, releases `SlotBondZnn`, and transitions `PegOutRecord` to `RELEASED` (re-pointable via `RepointTrappedPegOut` if another operator's substitution trapped `outpoint_d`!).
3. **Reachability-Checked Reimbursement & Staggered-CLTV BitVM2 Payout**: Winning operator `m` calls **`ClaimReimbursementSlot(outboxId, operatorId_m, roundId)`** (verifies `epoch(roundId)` is in `VaultUtxoSet[outpoint_d].epochEntries` with `m`'s bit set, `roundId` is registered & unused on an unretired lane, AND serial kickoff feasibility `max(now, lastClaimedTs + 5.5d) <= anchorCutoffTs = E_anchor_ts + 46d`, consuming its lane reservation; recoverable via `ResetFailedReimbursementSlot` if the claimed round expires/burns without operator fraud!) and broadcasts `RoundKickoffTx_{m,l,r}` (`Bond = 3.70 BTC`, per-field checksummed `OP_HASH160` `w=4` W-OTS `W_kick`).
   - Unchallenged (`T_happy = 144`): `Take1Tx_{d,m,l,r}` (`Out 2 <pk_m>` CPFP) pays `0.99899 BTC` to `ReimburseEscrow^{T1}` (72-block CSV OR `WrongDepositDisproveTx^{T1}` routing `0.99899 BTC` to `QUARANTINED` `RecoveryVaultUTXO_d`, burning `2.70 BTC` bond, paying watcher `1.00 BTC - fee`; verifier-decided SMT inclusion/absence on `BTC_VAULT_SPEND_PROOF` across `ACTIVE`/`RELEASED`/`QUARANTINED` keeps registered Takes in `PENDING_TRAP` for `230` blocks, mirrored via `SyncPendingTrapVault`!) AND `0.00100 BTC` to Reserve.
   - Challenged (`ChallengeTx`, `Out 0 = F_assert = 1.45 BTC`, terminal sink): Under Assumptions #2 & #3, operator posts `AssertStartTx` (`288`) → parallel **`AssertCertDATx`** (`29,882 vB → 30 kvB` derived per-block `OP_HASH160` `w=4` W-OTS, `3` stack-safe inputs publishing the fixed `408 B` `targetCert || handoffSlot` across `7` independently-checksummed SHA-256 blocks + `6` midstates = `1,239` digits, verified by `7` stack-safe Leaf-3B chunks `<= 265` digits, `C = 1,208`!) + parallel `AssertChunkTx_{1..32}` (`+72`) → `AssertEndTx` (`+72`). Any failure triggers `DisproveTx` / `BurnBond*Tx` (`SelfSlash <= -0.20 / -0.40 BTC`, `HonestOp >= +0.195 BTC` @ `500 sat/vB`, break-even `289 kvB`), PLUS `BTC_OPERATOR_SLASH_PROOF` slashes `100%` of Operator `m`'s `>= 31,500 ZNN` bond!
   - **Closed Rotation & Staggered CLTV Exit (`R5-H3`, `R5-L3`, `R6-L4`)**: Gen `g+1` is registered by **day `32`** (`E_anchor(g+1) = E_anchor(g) + 1`), all live vaults (`UNASSIGNED`, `ASSIGNED`, `QUARANTINED`, AND un-claimed `RELEASED`) are re-graphed onto `g+1` via `VAULT_GRAPH_EPOCH_ADDED` (`0x0D`) by **day `43`** (`QuarantineExpiredVault` unlocks at `T_kickoff_cutoff + 60h = day 48.5` so finalized batches never race quarantine!); operators exit gen `g` bonds cleanly via `RoundRetireTx` (syncing `BTC_ROUND_RETIRED` `0x10`) during `[day 51.5, day 58)`; `StaleConnB/CKillTx` locks out Takes at **`T_kill_expiry = day 56`** and `StaleChainBurnTx` burns unretired bonds at **`T_bond_expiry = day 58`** (`288 > 72` blocks after `T_kill_expiry` so traps can never be pre-empted!).
4. **Non-Delivery Refund**: If no `FrontingTx` is admitted before `checkpointH = timeoutHeight + 558` reaches `K_pilot = 14`, `BTC_NON_DELIVERY_PROOF` emits `FOREIGN_NON_DELIVERY`; `MINTED_REFUND_V1` returns `D_bucket` `zBTC` to `rec.RefundRecipient`, releases the reservation, and re-enqueues `outpoint_d` ONLY if `VaultUtxoSet[outpoint_d].status == ASSIGNED` (never overwriting `PENDING_TRAP`, `RECOVERED`, or `QUARANTINED`!).

## 4. Honest End-to-End Security & Threat Model (5 Explicit Tier 1A Assumptions — `R5-L5`)

**Tier 1A BitVM2 vaults are NOT trustless.** Delivered security is bounded by the weakest of **FIVE** explicit Tier 1A assumptions (plus optimistic claim admission):

1. **Zenon 30-Day BLS12-381 Finality Committee Honesty & Reachable Slashing Backstop (`R6-H1`, `R6-H2`, `G-23`)**: A colluding `>= 21`-of-`30` (`> 2/3` weight) Pillar supermajority in any 30-day finality epoch from `E_anchor` onward can forge a `FinalityCertificate` (or forged handoff). Deterred and bounded (subject to Assumption #3 challenging invalid kickoffs) by three coupled mechanisms:
   - **Mandatory Bitcoin Certificate Publication (`AssertCertDATx`, `29,882 vB → 30 kvB` derived)**: On any challenge, the operator MUST publish the fixed `408 B` `targetCert || handoffSlot` (`0x00^204` when no handoff exists) across `7` independently-checksummed `OP_HASH160` `w=4` W-OTS SHA-256 blocks + `6` midstates (`1,239` digits), verified by `7` stack-safe Leaf-3B chunks (`<= 265` digits/leaf, `C = 1,208`).
   - **Historical-Root Full-Preimage Slashing (`R6-H1`)**: `PillarContract.SubmitInvalidCertProof(cert)` verifies the BLS12-381 signature under the **historical Tag-15 committee matching `cert.FinalityCommitteeRoot`** (NOT merely `canonicalSigningCommitteeRoot(cert.Height)`!), then compares ALL 7 certificate fields against canonical Zenon state at `cert.Height` (with `chainId` bound in the signing digest). Because `CarryOverCertificate` is deleted, ANY committee-`E`-signed forged handoff or target at ANY height in epoch `E+1` (transition OR non-transition!) is slashable on Zenon when published on Bitcoin, economically enforcing the handoff pin without requiring an in-circuit oracle!
   - **Staggered `56d`/`58d` Bitcoin CLTV Expiry + U2 `72`-Day Post-Service Stake Lock (`A-PILLAR-1`)**: Every `RoundChain` expires on Bitcoin by `day 58`, while every Pillar's `15,000 ZNN` collateral stays locked and slashable for `72` days after its last finality epoch (`>= 58d + 14d` grace, independent of per-Pillar `% 90d` revoke phase), backing the Governance supply cap (`MaxRepresentationSupply * SpotPrice <= 157,500 ZNN`, weekly Governance check + Guardian TWAP trigger; pilot `15 BTC` @ `ASSUMED 10,500 ZNN/BTC`). *(Note: unchallenged `Take1Tx` forgeries do not publish certs — prevented by Assumption #3!)*
2. **1-of-N Live Verifier Committee Honesty (`Q_graph`, `N >= 7` Pilot)**: At least **1 of $N$** independent `Q_graph` verifier members remains honest **in perpetuity** (never signing unauthorized vault/connector spends, enforcing Phase-1-before-Phase-2 ceremony order, signing accurate release transcripts, and co-signing 30-day round-chain rotations & recovery re-graphing). *(Per-round keys `Q_round` ARE deleted at round setup. Permanent loss of 1-of-N `Q_graph` member stops new rotations and recovery re-graphing; existing vaults remain redeemable through reachable active rounds prior to `T_kickoff_cutoff` under `G-21`, after which Governance Wind-Down applies.)*
3. **1-of-N Altruistic, Capitalized Bitcoin Watcher Liveness**: Against a rational fraudulent operator who self-slashes (`-0.20..-0.40 BTC` burn vs `-2.25..-3.70 BTC` vs watcher) via private miner submission — OR under third-party miner MEV (M-03) — challenging has realistic expected value `-(F_assert + f_chal) = -1.4600 BTC` @ `500 sat/vB`. Safety explicitly assumes **at least 1 altruistic / stakeholder-funded watcher** with liquid capital `>= 1.46 BTC` per concurrently-fraudulent lane (`14.60 BTC` worst-case simultaneous fraud across all 10 pilot lanes, plus small tx fee for `StaleConn{B,C}Kill` fallback). Operator fraud is strictly deterred by Bitcoin burn (`-0.20..-2.70 BTC` even self-slashed) PLUS `100%` slashing of `OperatorBond_ZNN >= 31,500 ZNN` (`>= 3.0 BTC` equivalent) + deactivation of all lanes!
