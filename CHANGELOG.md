## Executive Summary of `v0` → `v1` Architecture Shift

`v1` (`v3.0.0-rfc`) completely replaces `v0`'s unviable BitVM2 + rotating-PoS-committee hybrid — **while explicitly REJECTING custodial `k`-of-`n` federated multisigs** — with a **Zero-Federation, Zero-Soft-Fork, Zero-ZNN-Custody-Coupling Phased Architecture**:

1. **Explicit Rejection of Federated Multisig Custody:** Custodial `k`-of-`n` multisig federations fail Bitcoin's non-custodial ethos, fail Bob's partial-recovery test under `49%` Zenon DPoS splits, and fail Mike's zero-signer `0.25 BTC` exit test completely. `v1` forbids federated custody normatively.
2. **Stage 0 (`0–6 months`, `0 BTC` bridge custody risk — Mandatory Start):** Ships the **Direct-on-L1 Bitcoin SPV Event Oracle** (`Verify Bitcoin, Don't Bridge It.md`, strictly Event Validity, 1-/2-block SHA-256 coinbase midstate padding) + **non-custodial PTLC/LN atomic swaps** (`2.3×` June 2021 slow-block safe, absolute Unix timestamps) + full `go-zenon` `SetPlasmaVariables` pinning while building & auditing Stage 1A on Signet.
3. **Stage 1A Primary Path — Bitcoin-Anchored Sovereign ZK Validity Rollup + Right-Sized `BitVMX` (`n = 8`, `M = 3–5`, `L = 1`, `N = 7`):**
   - **0% Zenon Trust (`49%–100%` Compromise Proof):** Demotes Zenon to an untrusted feeless sequencing & soft-preconfirmation layer (`AccountBlock.Data`, `~28,480` Plasma, `0` sat BTC fee, honest `8 MiB` `~1,050–1,200 tx/s` ceiling). Canonical ordering, complete mutated-leaf DA (`0x02 BATCH_DA` + embedded `π_batch`), and uncensorable forced inclusion (`0x01 FORCED_EXIT`, `0x03 FORCED_TRANSFER`) live on Bitcoin L1 via full `≤ 1 MB` stripped-block scanning.
   - **Zero Standing Keys & Zero `ZNN` Custody Coupling:** Hard-pinned immutable `vk_STF` eliminates 30-day PoS rotations; ephemeral `Q_setup_d` and `Q_lane` keys are permanently deleted after setup (`OP_DROP` acyclic `DepositTx`, `{0.50, 0.25, 0.25}` BTC multi-tier vaults, `N`-of-`N` voluntary cohort handover only).
   - **Low-Cap `n = 8` `BitVMX-CPU` (`RV32IM`) Bisection (`12.8–18.8 BTC` Total Capital):** Cuts on-chain dispute size `~15×` vs BitVM2 (`~7.44 kvB`/prover tx, **100% BIP-431 TRUC v3** with `P2A` anchors; `20` steps covering `2^27 ≈ 134M` RV32IM instructions; `~75 kvB` Prover / `~15 kvB` Verifier; `F_chal = 0.40 BTC` covering `500 sat/vB` sustained, `533 sat/vB` break-even).
   - **Zero Early Vault Theft, Collusion-Proof Positive-EV Verifiers & Atomic `ExitSlotUTXO`:** Simultaneous `[0, 144]` challenge window, `T_take = 1,152 > 1,104`, `T_cooldown = 1,296`, `RetireStakeTx = 1,440`; dual-input `DisproveTx`/`TimeoutSlashTx` spends BOTH `Stake_r` AND `VaultConnector_r` (`0` BTC stolen, `0.15 BTC` L1 winner payout, `1.85 BTC` burned, `0.55 BTC` `B_zbtc` split guaranteeing `≥ +0.0167 BTC` positive EV for every honest verifier even under `6-of-7` verifier collusion!); 256-bit Lamport `TakeTx` (`~3.1 kvB`) eliminates `ReimburseEscrow`/`RecoveryVault`; `(2M + 1)`-leaf registered-operator spend-or-refund `ExitSlotUTXO` kills double-fronting and grief freezes.
4. **Stage 1B Explicit Alternative Gate (`clArk`):** If `< 5–10 BTC` budget or `3–6 mo` launch target, deploy `Second bark`/`Ark Labs arkd` (`2–5 BTC` active liquidity, `0` bonds) rather than under-bonding `BitVMX`/`BitVM2`, transparently relinquishing pooled fungible `zBTC` for discrete expiring VTXOs.
5. **Stage 2 & Appendix A (Track B Pure Tier-0 Soft-Fork Covenant):** Zero deleted-key L1 rollover; identical-STF `BitVMX` scaling via `N`-of-`N` Cohort 1 handover; custody backend swaps (including Track B direct-UTXO `OP_CCV + OP_TXHASH + OP_VERIFY_STARK` or `OP_CAT` covenants with zero header scanning) launch clean non-commingled `zBTC_v2`.
